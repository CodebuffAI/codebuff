# Privacy

Openbuff is a local-first BYOK fork, meaning all LLM inference runs directly against your configured providers via `openbuff.json`. Here's how data is handled.

## No Backend — Your Keys, Your Providers

Openbuff routes requests directly from your machine to your configured model providers (e.g. Anthropic, OpenAI, Google). There is no Openbuff backend that sees your code or conversations.

## No 3rd-Party Training on Your Data

Configure only AI model providers that **do not train on your data** (e.g. by using non-training API tiers).

## Local Logging

Openbuff stores conversation history locally at `~/.config/manicode/projects/`. Nothing is sent to Openbuff servers — there are none.

## Provider Privacy

Your data is subject to the privacy policies of whichever providers you configure in `openbuff.json`. We recommend reviewing each provider's data handling policies.

## Questions?

If you have questions about Openbuff's privacy approach, reach out to us at hello@openbuff.dev.
